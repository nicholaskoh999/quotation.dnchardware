PRICING — UNCHANGED THIS ROUND
------------------------------
The pricing formula, Auto Round, the weight formula, Cost Rate, Additional
Cost, Markup and Previous Price are byte-for-byte unchanged by the closing
repair. The manually verified case still answers what it answered:

    M24 x 1000 · 3.5513 kg/pc · Cost Rate 2.80 · Additional Cost 0.60
    Markup 4%   ->  3.5513 x 2.80 = 9.9436  + 0.60 = 10.5436
                    + 4% = 10.9653   Auto Round  =>  RM 10.97

Evidence frame F states that calculation on one screen and FAILS the run if the
answer ever stops being RM 10.97.

PRICING — UNCHANGED BY THE WORKFLOW POLISH ROUND TOO
----------------------------------------------------
The polish round separated three editing mechanisms, added row selection and
fixed four defects. It did not touch the pricing formula, the weight formula,
Auto Round, Cost Rate, Additional Cost, Markup, the diameter tables or the
parser. The one change that comes NEAR pricing is F31, and it removes a false
claim rather than a number: a row bulk-changed off the identity its historical
record described stops crediting that record. The rates the record contributed
stay on the row, because by then they are the row's own pricing entry.
