## Phase 0 — read-before-write findings

1. **iOS PWA viewport.** `index.php`: `viewport-fit=cover`, `apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style: default`,
   `theme-color #5b7af5` (old blue header). `companies.php`: no `viewport-fit`. Nothing anywhere uses
   `safe-area-inset-top`; the phone top bar `.rsp-top` is `position:sticky; top:var(--banner-h)`, 58px.
   Body background is the grey canvas `#e8ecf3`. Chromium at 390/430/844 shows the bar at y=0, no gap.
   iOS 26.1+ Home Screen apps draw a solid band between the status bar and the page that the page cannot
   reach, report no inset for it, and fill it from the page background (WebKit bug 301994, reopened, unresolved),
   which matches "a large blank grey area" over a white bar.
2. **Header / bottom navigation.** `<600px`: compact top bar + 4-item bottom nav (`.rsp-nav`, fixed, safe-area
   bottom padding), the quotation bar swaps with it (`data-rsp-bottom`). `.main` pads for both. 600–1023: drawer.
3. **Saved.** `html,body{overflow-x:hidden}` on BOTH made body a scroll container: the sticky top bar scrolled away
   (measured `top = -110px` after scrolling). Helper text "Select a company on the left" is shown at every width; the
   list is stacked ABOVE at ≤980px.
4. **Refresh / dirty state.** No pull-to-refresh in an installed iOS app. Dirty signals that exist:
   `savedQuoteDirty`, `quoteItems`, `hasMeaningfulDraft(buildUnsavedDraft())`, `rspEntryDirty`,
   `productEntryTouchedFields`, `editingItemIndex`, `editingQuoteId`, `pendingUnsavedDraft`,
   `draftRestoreInProgress`, `draftRecoveryChecked`; Quick Add `#wqaModal.open`, `wqa.aiBusy` / `wqa.busy`;
   `.modal-overlay.open`; `rspCur`. Existing loaders (`loadCompaniesCache`, `refreshDPRules`, `loadCompanies`)
   REPLACE caches with `[]` on failure, so they are not safe to reuse for a refresh as-is.
5. **Accessory pricing / rounding.** `resolvePriceMode`: Auto Round / No Round round the BOLT, accessories
   (`accAddon`, nut qty×price + fw qty×price + custom) are added after; Manual = typed customer price, accessories
   come out of it. Every product calc, Quick Add, history reuse and the saved-item recalc follow this.
   Contract written in `docs/control/PROJECT-GUARDRAILS.md` (ACCESSORIES, Stage 0B `98a31e3`) and owner-locked in
   `tests/suites/51-auto-round-rule.test.js` (`a173510`): "the combined final is never rounded again".
6. **Quick Add PDF restrictions.** Client: images ≤5 OR one PDF (`wqaOnePdf`), 10 MB image / 20 MB PDF. Server
   (`ai_validate_uploads`): ≤5 images, "either images or one PDF", PDF ≤20 MB / ≤10 pages. Production web PHP
   limits measured earlier: upload_max 2M, post_max 8M — several PDFs in ONE POST would hit them first.
7. **Sonnet PDF transport.** One native base64 `document` block per request (`ai_anthropic.php`), one Luna
   fallback on technical failure, always Review.
8. **Product filtering.** No supported-product filter beyond the extraction schema's products; unsupported
   document words error, mixed documents keep the rows that resolve. The model ignores non-fastener drawings on its own.

## Remaining risks / limits

- iOS standalone cannot be reproduced on this Windows test bench (Chromium, no safe areas). Session A is the root-
  cause-consistent fix (band colour), not a removal of the band itself, which iOS draws. Owner smoke on the
  installed app decides.
- Pull-to-refresh uses a non-passive `touchmove` listener (work only while a pull is armed); verified with
  dispatched touches, not a real finger on iOS.
- Multi-PDF: a per-file note given to one PDF is sent to that PDF only, and the review keeps the existing
  "check each row against its file" warning; per-file notes are not re-applied client-side per file.
- Multi-PDF costs one Sonnet call per PDF (≈ $0.02–0.06 each).
